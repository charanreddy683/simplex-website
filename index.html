Frequently asked questions

Clarity at the start of every engagement.

Every program is tailored. These answers cover the practical foundations of working with Simplex AI Labs.

How do projects begin?

We start with discovery to understand your data, quality requirements, workflows and delivery objectives, then establish a focused pilot.

What industries do you support?

We support complex AI initiatives across healthcare, mobility, retail, manufacturing, logistics, finance, public sector and other data-intensive domains.

How do you ensure quality?

Multi-layer review, expert guidance, random audits, feedback loops and AI-assisted validation are built into every workflow.

Can you sign NDAs?

Yes. We can put appropriate confidentiality arrangements in place before project discussions or data review.

Can you scale annotation teams?

Yes. Dedicated teams and flexible delivery models let us grow capacity while maintaining workflow consistency and quality controls.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing reflects the complexity of the data, required expertise, quality standards, volume, turnaround and engagement model.

What file formats do you support?

We work with common image, video, audio, text, point cloud and structured-data formats, with requirements confirmed during discovery.

How long does a pilot project take?

Timing depends on scope and data readiness. A focused pilot is designed to quickly validate workflow, quality and delivery expectations.
